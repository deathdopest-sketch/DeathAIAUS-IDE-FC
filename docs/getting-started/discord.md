# Discord Server Setup Guide

## Suggested Channel Structure

### 📢 Information Channels

- **#announcements** (read-only) - Updates, releases
- **#getting-started** - Installation help, first-time setup
- **#roadmap** - What's coming next, feature requests

### 💬 Support Channels

- **#general** - General chat
- **#help** - Technical support, troubleshooting
- **#feasibility-reports** - Users share wild feasibility warnings they got
- **#success-stories** - Projects that worked out
- **#horror-stories** - When other AI tools failed them (cathartic)

### 🛠️ Development Channels

- **#feature-requests** - What users want
- **#bug-reports** - What's broken
- **#contributions** - For developers helping improve it
- **#model-recommendations** - Which models work best

### 🎨 Community Building

- **#share-your-projects** - What people are building
- **#ai-tool-rants** - Place to vent about overpromising AI (builds solidarity)

## Community Growth Strategy

### Launch Announcement Locations

1. **r/programming**
2. **r/learnprogramming**
3. **r/opensource**
4. **Hacker News**
5. **Dev.to**

### Engagement Tips

- Include Discord link in README and all posts
- Engage authentically - your story resonates
- People who've been burned by AI tools will find you
- Early adopter benefits - People who join now and contribute get recognition
- Weekly dev logs - Keep community updated on progress

### Your Story is Your Marketing

> "I got burned by AI tools that promised the world. Built something that tells the truth instead."

That resonates. People are tired of overhyped AI. You're offering the antidote.
