### 4.1 Your Code Stays Local

**100% Local Processing:**
- All code editing happens on YOUR machine
- AI models run locally (via LM Studio or direct .gguf files)
- Your source code **NEVER leaves your computer**

### 4.2 AI Model Privacy

**Local AI Models:**
- You can use any local AI model (LM Studio, direct .gguf)

- No data sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, or other cloud providers

We do NOT use your proprietary code or prompts to train AI models. Your code is yours alone.
